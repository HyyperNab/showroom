Six languages. One segment. No cultural barriers.

An established private practice with 20 years of experience in ADHD diagnostics, microbiome analysis, preventive screening, and allergology wanted to systematically build its adult segment. The practice had an international patient base (including a dedicated Japanese phone line) but no platform that reflected this.

1 · The Architecture

The solution was a platform with 6 language modes (EN, DE, FR, JP, AR, ES), switchable by a single click. Each language offers the full patient journey: services, ADHD clinic, microbiome diagnostics, executive medicine, and nutritional counseling.

2 · The Business Model

The clinic operated on two previously closed days (Tuesday and Wednesday, 09:00–16:00). A specialist in internal medicine brought his own adult segment: executive and performance medicine, adult ADHD, gut-brain axis, refractory gastrointestinal conditions, and sleep medicine. The practice added one medical assistant for the new days. The infrastructure was already there — it was made productive.

3 · The Bridge

The most critical bridge was the transition of adolescent ADHD patients into adult care. The practice had 20 years of pediatric ADHD experience; the new adult specialist absorbed these patients seamlessly, instead of losing them to general practitioners.
