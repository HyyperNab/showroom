Global SaMD Governance

Regulatory strategy across three frameworks. No timelines stated — pathways are under evaluation per jurisdiction.

Compliance Architecture

Data Governance: Federated learning with differential privacy. Raw patient data remains at each institution. Only model gradients are shared via secure aggregation.

Human-in-the-Loop: AI operates in advisory mode during clinical validation. Deterministic safety floor overrides AI when confidence drops below threshold.

Post-Market: Automated event logging, periodic model audit, PMCF per MDR Annex XIV Part B. PCCP enables model updates without full resubmission.

Client identities, proprietary methods, and trade secrets have been removed or anonymized. No confidential information is disclosed. Applicable Law: This portfolio is governed by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation 2016/679), the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG), and applicable non-disclosure agreements executed between Dr. Nabil Ktari and the respective clients.
