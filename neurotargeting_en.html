2 · The Timing Principle

Signals outside the window are noise. A perfectly designed signal that arrives outside its receptivity window is ignored — the budget is burned. The architecture forces every signal to declare its window before it is sent.

Biorhythm is the clock. Budget allocation follows neural receptivity, not availability. The system concentrates spend in the hours when the brain is most receptive to the specific signal type.

Fatigue is computational. Audio signals habituate quickly. The architecture varies pitch, pauses, and ambient texture every 48–72 hours to maintain click-through rates.
