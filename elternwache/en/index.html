The Audit

Six points. Every visit.

Documented with photo and timestamp. Every observation, every piece of evidence, every action taken.

  1. The Fridge

    We check expiration dates and empty shelves. What is she actually eating, versus what she tells you on the phone.

  2. Clothing

    Condition, cleanliness, season. Is she still dressing properly.

  3. Hygiene

    Body, bathroom, household. Are there signs of neglect.

  4. Appointments

    Dentist, follow-up, physio, GP. Booked, missed, overdue.

  5. Mail

    Unopened post, warnings, authorities. Is a power of attorney in place.

  6. Agenda

    Who does what, where are the gaps, what's next.

  7. Meta-Service

    Everything else. The dog, the haircut, the weekly bath. No "not my job."
