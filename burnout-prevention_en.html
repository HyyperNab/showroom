1 · The Thesis

Every large organization runs two health systems. The first lives in policy documents — programs, standards, wellness offerings. The second lives in the data: aggregated absence patterns, anonymized screening trends, participation rates. Research and practice show that the two rarely match. The gap between them is the earliest measurable predictor of absence crises, visible months before the first long-term case.

2 · Three Principles

Measure the gap before treating the symptom. The unit of analysis is the discrepancy between intended and observed health. Gap first, program second.

Aggregated, never individual. Every signal operates at department level. Works-council co-determination is designed in from day one.

Every number is a hypothesis. Public benchmarks open the argument; the site's own data closes it.

3 · The 90-Day Pilot

PhaseGoalOutput
Week 1–4
Foundation		Data inventory: which streams exist, which are accessible at aggregate level, which legal basis applies. Works-council alignment briefing. Baseline gap measurement.Gap map: where policy intent and observed data diverge, ranked by signal strength and cost burden.
Week 5–8
Signal Architecture		KPI definition and validation; threshold logic; department-level signal thresholds with documented drift monitoring.Validated signal model with GREEN / YELLOW / RED definitions, ready for works-council review.
Week 9–12
Intervention		Two to three pilot departments with YELLOW / RED signals; appropriate intervention design; pre-registered evaluation metrics.First read-out: signal validity, intervention acceptance, effect estimate — with confidence intervals, not point estimates.

Signal Thresholds — Department Level Only

GREEN — Indicators within expected range → Standard monitoring YELLOW — One indicator drifting, two consecutive quarters → Structured review RED — Multiple indicators converging → Intervention team, 90-day recovery plan

No status triggers automated actions on individuals. Every YELLOW and RED is reviewed by occupational health professionals before an intervention is designed — human-in-the-loop is not a retrofitted feature, it is the operating model.
